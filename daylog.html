Daylog

Vibe-coded app · 2026

A little journal shared with your favourite people. One entry a day, two photos max. No likes, no streaks, no pressure.

The idea

Small circles

Every journal lives inside one small group of up to 12 friends. Nothing is public, ever.

One entry a day

Write a few lines, add up to two photos and pick how the day felt.

No streaks

The archive shows the days you felt like writing, not the ones you missed.
