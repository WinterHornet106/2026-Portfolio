Domino’s App Redesign

App redesign · 2026 · Figma

A refresh of the Domino’s app around what its reviews kept asking for: pay in the app, take fewer steps, and know when the pizza is on its way. A personal concept, not affiliated with Domino’s.

What reviewers said

Paying was a hassle

Reviewers asked why they couldn’t pay through the app, and noted there was no Apple Pay.

It looked dated

One review said the app looked like it was made in 2010.

Too many steps

Checking points or going back to the menu meant extra screens.

Where’s my pizza?

A reviewer wanted a heads-up when the pizza leaves the store.

“UI and design look like app was made in 2010. Doesn’t even have Apple Pay.”

App Store review

“Make it so when the pizza is leaving the store there is a notification.”

App Store review

What changed

Faster sign-in

Continue with Apple or Google, or use a passkey. No password to type.

Order in fewer taps

Reorder your last order from home; size, crust and extras on one screen; points shown in the bag.

Apple Pay and live tracking

Pay in the app, then follow the driver on a map and on your Lock Screen.

Points you can see

A progress ring shows how close the next free pizza is, and past orders reorder in one tap.
