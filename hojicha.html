Hojicha Mascot

Character & motion · 2026 · this site

The cup in my logo. I drew him in Figma, then rebuilt him as a posable rig so he moves live on the page. Give him a move.

How he works

From drawing to rig

I rebuilt my Figma drawing as one layered SVG, posed by four numbers: drink level, where he looks, his lids and his straw.

Real liquid

The tea is a small water simulation, so it sloshes when he leans or hops.

A beat behind

His eyes and straw sit on springs, so they lag and wobble after every move.

Five moods

Sway, glance, bounce, shimmy and doze. Hover the logo in the nav and he picks one, never the same twice.

A first-visit intro

On your first visit he drinks his tea, then flies into the corner of the page on real spring physics.
