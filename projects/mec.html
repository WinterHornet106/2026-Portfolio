An events app for a co-op coming home.

MEC Connect

An events app with Mountain Equipment Company to rebuild its community after five years of foreign ownership.

Role
Designer & researcher
Year
2025
Team
6 designers at SFU, with MEC’s product team
Client
Mountain Equipment Company

A co-op that lost
its sense of community.

MEC was a Canadian co-op until 2020, when it was sold to a US private equity firm without asking its members. Canadian ownership returned in 2025. The trust didn’t.

Events were still MEC’s strongest link to members and sell out within hours, but they’re buried online and booked through a third-party tool. MEC now also needs them to prove their value.

Three patterns,
one we could act on.

In-store observation, then interviews with MEC’s product team, six staff and two customers.

A legacy trust gap

Long-time members felt the co-op values left with the ownership change.

Beginners don’t know where to start

Newcomers struggled to choose gear and assumed MEC was for pros.

A strong but hidden community

Events were scattered and hard to join, so loyal customers rarely met.

“We host events to keep people connected. Community is a big part of what MEC stands for.”

MEC store staff

The pivot

We started with Piper’s need: an app explaining each product’s sustainability. MEC pushed back. Nobody downloads an app that only educates, and verifying impact stats cost too much. So we switched to community, George’s need.

Co-design workshop with MEC

Demand isn’t the problem

Attendance hit a low, yet events sell out within hours. The limit is capacity.

“Nobody talked to me”

First-timers don’t return when they arrive alone or land at the wrong skill level.

Events have to earn their keep

MEC’s product lead suggested event-specific coupons to tie attendance to sales.

One home for MEC’s events,
and the people at them.

MEC Connect brings discovery, sign-up and community into one app MEC owns.

  • Discover

    Tuned at onboarding to your city, activities and skill level.

  • Know before you go

    Every event lists its agenda, skill level and what to bring.

  • Event communities

    Joining unlocks a space for host announcements and attendee posts, so nobody arrives alone.

  • Badges & coupons

    Each event earns a badge. Five unlock 10% off, ten unlock 20%, and MEC sees which events drive sales.

A real client,
not a hypothetical brief.

MEC’s feedback killed our first idea and their workshop shaped the second. We handed over the full flow, brand guidelines and a business case linking events to loyalty and sales, ready to build on its own or inside MEC’s planned membership app.
