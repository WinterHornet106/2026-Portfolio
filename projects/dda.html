2024 · Microsite & posters

Giving young designers the main stage.

Dutch Design Awards

A poster series and event microsite that spotlights the Young Designers category at the Dutch Design Awards.

Role
Visual designer
Year
2024
Team
Student brief, 5 designers at SFU
Surface
Posters, web prototype

Eight categories,
one overlooked.

The Dutch Design Awards honour Dutch design across eight categories. Young Designers works differently: nominees submit a whole portfolio instead of one project, the category comes with a grant, and it’s often overlooked.

We framed a microsite that runs during the nine-day event, giving visitors the schedule and category details while giving young nominees a larger audience.

Posters first,
then the web.

We built the visual system on posters before touching the site, testing three lines of investigation drawn from Ellen Lupton’s design principles. Early rounds were too literal; the final three balanced image, type and space.

Image as figure-ground

One photo cropped and repeated at different sizes forms a new shape on the page.

Visible grids

Grid lines divide the page into sections and stitch clusters back together.

A pattern, broken

Fragments form a pattern, and the headline sits exactly where it breaks.

A functional grid,
not an expressive one.

We tested an expressive layout against a functional one. The functional grid matched the posters and gave clearer direction, so the whole site runs on it, carrying over the visible grid, duotone images and monospace body type.

Image on hover

On the all-nominees list, hovering a row previews that designer’s work beside it.

Years that preview

Hovering a year enlarges it and plays a clip from each nominee before you commit.

Spotlight or event info

A single click moves visitors between the designers and practical event details.

Three posters,
one working site.

Every page carries the poster system: a homepage, nominees by year, every past nominee, the nine-day schedule and the category’s history.
