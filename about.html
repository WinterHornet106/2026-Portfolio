I’m Jay, a product designer in Vancouver. I make digital experiences that feel clear and easy to use, balancing usability, visual storytelling and real behaviour.

I like work that sits between design and strategy: interfaces that solve problems, not just look polished. I’ve worked across UI/UX, branding, research and prototyping, taking concepts from early ideas to working flows.

Outside design: photos, markets and the next idea worth building.

Three principles
I actually design by.

  1. Build early, learn quickly, improve faster.

    A rough clickable flow teaches more than weeks of discussion.

  2. Design for real people, not assumptions.

    Research and honest feedback show how people actually think and hesitate, which the screen alone can’t.

  3. Obsess over the details others skip.

    Spacing, wording, states and transitions: the small decisions shape the whole experience.
