Digital Macro Switch

Mac app · 2026 · Swift & SceneKit

A 3D mechanical keyboard switch that lives on my Mac desktop. Click it for a real switch sound and a tap of trackpad haptics.

0 clicks

Rendered from the app. Try both keys; the sounds come from its sound packs.

What it does

Sound

Real switch recordings, picked per pack.

  • Clicky
  • Tactile
  • Linear
  • Import your own

Look

Change the keycap and the base.

  • Any colour
  • Your own icon
  • 1 or 2 keys
  • 3 sizes

Actions

Each key can run something.

  • Single click
  • Double click
  • Triple click
  • Fidget mode

Always there

Sits above every window.

  • Full-screen apps
  • Send to back
  • ⌃⌥S to summon
  • Haptics
