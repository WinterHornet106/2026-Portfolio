Mac app · 2026 · Swift

Switch Fidget

A 3D mechanical keyboard switch that lives on my Mac desktop. Click it for a real switch sound and a tap of trackpad haptics.

0 clicks

Rendered from the app. Try both keys; the sounds come from its sound packs.

Pick a sound

Sound packs for different switches, and you can import your own.

Make it yours

Any keycap colour, your own icon on top, or a slime keycap. One switch or two.

Clicks that do things

A single, double or triple click can run an action. Fidget mode pauses them so it just clicks.

Character & motion · 2026 · this site

Hojicha

The cup in my logo. I drew him in Figma, then rebuilt him as a posable rig so he moves live on the page. Give him a move.

Real liquid

The tea is a small water simulation, so it sloshes when he leans or hops.

A beat behind

His eyes and straw sit on springs, so they lag and wobble after every move.

A first-visit intro

On your first visit he drinks his tea, then flies into the corner of the page.
